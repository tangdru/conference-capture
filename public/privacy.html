Privacy Policy

Conference Capture · Last updated November 8, 2026

Conference Capture helps you take notes, photos and a transcript during a talk, and turn them into a presentation afterwards. This policy explains what the app collects, where it goes and how to delete it. Conference Capture is made by Andrew Tang. Contact: tangdru@gmail.com.

What the app stores

  • Your account. An account ID, and your email address if you add one so you can sign in again.
  • What you capture. Session titles, your notes, photos and videos you take, the text read from slide photos, the transcript of the talk, the moments you mark, and the presentations the app builds.
  • Your settings. Your presentation options.

These are stored on our servers (Supabase) so they sync to your account. They are private to your account.

Audio

The talk is transcribed on your iPhone. The app does not upload recordings of the room; only the transcript text is stored with your session. The short audio clips saved when you tap Remember this stay on your phone.

AI processing

To build presentations, name sessions, summarise and add context, and answer your questions, the app sends the relevant session content (transcript, notes, slide text, photos and your question) to Anthropic’s Claude API. Anthropic processes it to return a result and does not use it to train its models. See Anthropic’s privacy policy.

Who else sees your data

  • Supabase hosts the app’s database, file storage and sign-in. See Supabase’s privacy policy.
  • Anthropic processes content for the AI features above.
  • People you share with. When you share a presentation, you send them a copy of the file.

We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking. Nothing is shared with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Camera, microphone and photos

The app asks before using the camera (to photograph slides), the microphone (to transcribe the talk and save clips), speech recognition (on your iPhone), and your photo library (only to save photos when you ask). You can change these any time in the Settings app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleting a session removes it and its photos, videos and presentation. To delete everything, open the menu and tap Delete account: your account, sessions, photos, videos, presentations and settings are permanently deleted from our servers, and the app’s data is cleared from your phone. Copies of presentations you’ve already shared aren’t affected. You can also email tangdru@gmail.com to ask for deletion or a copy of your data.

Children

Conference Capture isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
