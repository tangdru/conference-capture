Conference Capture support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email tangdru@gmail.com, or tap Send feedback in the app’s menu. Please include your build number (shown at the bottom of the menu).

Getting started

Tap New Session when a talk begins. Take photos of slides, type notes, and tap the star at key moments. Afterwards, swipe left on the session and build its presentation. Open the menu and tap Tutorial for a walkthrough.

Signing in on another phone

Add your email from the menu (Account → Add email). Then you can sign in on any phone with a code sent to that address, and all your sessions come with you.

Deleting your account

Open the menu and tap Delete account. Everything is permanently deleted. See the privacy policy for details.
